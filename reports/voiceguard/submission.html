VoiceGuard: measured evidence

Free local CPU application · AASIST-L + trained adaptation · English / Hindi / Tamil exploratory coverage

Deterministic subsets, not complete benchmark evaluation. Thresholds fixed on development data. Multilingual adaptation uses external IndicTTS training speech, so this is a custom research protocol, not an official ASVspoof challenge submission. Acoustic authenticity does not establish caller identity or scam intent.

Training

Frozen official AASIST-L 160-dimensional embedding + 88 LFCC statistics; trained PyTorch 248-64-1 MLP. Encoder weights unchanged. Selected using development data only. 2492 clean/augmented training windows; 245 development clips; 53 epochs executed.
